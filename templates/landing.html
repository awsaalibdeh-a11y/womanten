The lock that makes you earn your scrolls

On Android, Womanten can lock the apps you scroll too much. Open one and it asks for a few reps first. It watches through your camera, on your phone, and never saves or sends the video.

Pick your appsTikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or any app on your phone. Choose the hours it is on.
Open one and it stops youYou are sent home and the camera opens with your exercise.
Do the repsPush-ups, squats, jumping jacks or a plank hold. The camera counts them.
Earn your scrollsEach rep earns 10 scrolls, which you can change. When they run out, it locks again.

Focus mode goes further: pick 10 to 180 minutes and your locked apps are blocked completely. To leave early you have to do 10 reps on camera.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Get the Android app and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Womanten and choose Lock apps. Turn on Womanten app lock in Accessibility settings.
  4. Pick your apps and your exercise. Allow the camera when it asks.

On your Windows PC

The Windows app locks distracting programs and websites in your browser (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and more) until you earn time with reps on your webcam. It lives in the tray, sends gentle reminders, and has Focus mode.

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Open Womanten.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, press More info, then Run anyway. The app is new and not signed yet.
  3. Right-click the tray icon and choose Lock settings to pick what to lock and which exercise unlocks it.

Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no account and no payment. The AI features use a small amount of AI each time, so very heavy use in one day may be limited.

Does it work on iPhone or Mac?

The website works on any phone or computer, and you can add it to your home screen. The app lock needs Android or Windows, because iPhone and Mac do not let an app lock other apps.

How does the lock know I did a push-up?

Your camera and an on-device pose model watch your arms. It counts a rep when you lower your body and push back up. It is not perfect, so go slowly and keep your arms in view.

Can I cheat the lock?

You can turn it off in Android's Accessibility settings. That is on purpose: it is a promise you keep to yourself, and it works best when you choose it.

Where is my data?

On your own phone or browser. The AI features send only what you ask about (like your question or your numbers for a review) to be answered, and nothing is sold. Use Back up your data to keep a copy.

Is it a replacement for a doctor or a therapist?

No. Womanten is a coach for everyday habits and confidence. For health, injuries, allergies or feeling very low, talk to a doctor, a parent or a trusted adult.
